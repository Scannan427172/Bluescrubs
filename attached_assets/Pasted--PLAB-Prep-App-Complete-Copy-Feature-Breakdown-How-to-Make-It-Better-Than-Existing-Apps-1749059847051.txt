🩺 PLAB Prep App – Complete Copy & Feature Breakdown

🔧 How to Make It Better Than Existing Apps

1. Adaptive Learning System

📈 Like a private tutor in your pocket.

	•	Tracks your strengths and weaknesses
	•	Adjusts daily study content automatically
	•	Boosts low-performing areas (e.g. cardiology, ethics)
	•	Smart revision reminders based on spaced repetition

⸻

2. Full PLAB Journey Support

🚑 Go beyond questions. Guide users through the full path.

	•	PLAB 1 & 2 booking walkthroughs
	•	Visa and travel guidance for international candidates
	•	GMC registration steps post-exam
	•	NHS job interview prep
	•	Motivational check-ins and burnout prevention alerts

⸻

3. Realistic PLAB 2 OSCE Simulation

🎥 The most immersive OSCE prep experience yet.

	•	Video-based simulated stations (actor-patient style)
	•	Record your own responses for self-review
	•	AI feedback on communication, structure, empathy
	•	Filter stations by theme (e.g. abdominal pain, mental health)

⸻

4. Built-In Planner & Tracker

🗓️ A smart study plan that adapts to your life.

	•	Weekly planner with drag-and-drop topics
	•	Auto-generates daily goals based on your exam date
	•	“Missed a day” function to instantly replan
	•	Well-being tips (hydration, breaks, sleep nudges)

⸻

5. Peer Community + Mentor Support

🤝 Don’t study alone. Learn from those ahead of you.

	•	Forums by PLAB 1, PLAB 2, country, or study stage
	•	Verified mentors who passed recently
	•	Ask-an-expert anonymously
	•	Share your mock scores with your study group

⸻

6. Cultural & NHS Context Training

🇬🇧 Succeed beyond the exam.

	•	Modules on NHS hierarchy and referral systems
	•	Breaking bad news the UK way
	•	Consultation etiquette and time management
	•	Accents, phrases, and pronunciation tips
	•	Cultural scenario quizzes

⸻

7. Offline & Low-Data Mode

🌍 Perfect for users in low-bandwidth areas.

	•	Download mocks and flashcards offline
	•	“Lite Mode” strips videos and loads faster
	•	Offline practice mode with result syncing when back online
