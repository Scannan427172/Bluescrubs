🩺 What is PLAB?

PLAB (Professional and Linguistic Assessments Board) is a licensing exam for international medical graduates (IMGs) who want to practise medicine in the UK under the General Medical Council (GMC).

It ensures that overseas doctors have the skills, knowledge, and English ability equivalent to a UK medical graduate who is ready for Foundation Year 2 (F2).

⸻

📚 Structure of the PLAB Exam

PLAB 1
	•	Format: 180 multiple-choice questions (MCQs)
	•	Duration: 3 hours
	•	Style: Each question has a scenario with five options; only one correct
	•	Content: Common clinical scenarios from UK practice – diagnosis, treatment, and next steps
	•	Held: In multiple countries worldwide (including the UK)

PLAB 2
	•	Format: 18 objective structured clinical examination (OSCE) stations
	•	Duration: Each station is 8 minutes
	•	Style: You interact with actors and assessors; simulates real-world doctor–patient scenarios
	•	Location: Only in Manchester, UK
	•	Content: Communication, diagnosis, decision-making, empathy, ethics, and examination skills

⸻

✅ Eligibility Requirements

You must have:
	•	A primary medical qualification accepted by the GMC
	•	IELTS (minimum 7.5 overall, 7.0 in each section) or OET (Grade B in each domain)
	•	12 months of internship/clinical experience post-graduation (for full registration)

⸻

📆 How to Apply
	1.	Register with GMC Online
	2.	Pass English language test (IELTS/OET)
	3.	Book PLAB 1 (available worldwide)
	4.	Pass PLAB 1 to be eligible for PLAB 2
	5.	Pass PLAB 2, then apply for GMC registration with a license to practise

⸻

📘 Preparation Tips
	•	Use official GMC blueprints to guide your study
	•	PLAB 1 resources: PLABable, Samson Notes, Pastest
	•	PLAB 2 resources: Practise mock OSCEs, communication skills, online academies (e.g., Aspire, Swamy, Road to UK)
	•	Join IMG forums or Telegram groups to get peer support and tips

⸻

💰 Costs (as of 2025)
	•	PLAB 1: £255
	•	PLAB 2: £934
	•	GMC registration after passing: ~£170
(Travel and accommodation for PLAB 2 in the UK is extra)

⸻

🏁 Final Outcome

After passing PLAB 1 & 2:
	•	You can apply for full GMC registration
	•	Start practising in the NHS
	•	Apply for Foundation Year 2 level jobs or higher, depending on your experience
