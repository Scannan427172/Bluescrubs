Ways to Make Your PLAB App Better Than Existing Ones

⸻

1. Adaptive Learning System

📈 Make it feel like a private tutor

	•	Use AI to track user strengths/weaknesses and dynamically adjust what topics they revise each day
	•	Example: If a user struggles with cardiology scenarios, the app boosts those questions and flashcards
	•	Include “Smart Reminders” to revise topics they’re forgetting (Spaced Repetition)

⸻

2. Full PLAB Journey Support — Not Just Questions

🚑 Most PLAB apps stop at content — you go further

Include:
	•	PLAB 1 & PLAB 2 booking guides, deadlines, and checklists
	•	UK visa application guidance (if needed)
	•	NHS induction prep
	•	GMC registration walkthrough after passing PLAB 2
	•	Daily motivational nudges and check-ins (burnout is real!)

⸻

3. Realistic PLAB 2 Practice Mode

🎥 OSCE prep that feels real — not flat scripts

	•	Include video-based simulated stations (patient talks, you respond)
	•	Optional AI feedback or score per station
	•	Let users record themselves and compare with ideal responses
	•	Include “stations by symptom” or theme (e.g., abdominal pain, breaking bad news)

⸻

4. Integrated Planner & Tracker

🗓️ So users stop bouncing between apps

	•	Drag-and-drop weekly planner for study
	•	Set exam dates → app auto-generates daily goals
	•	Include “I missed a day” feature to replan automatically
	•	Add health/reminder prompts: “Take a break,” “You’ve been studying for 2 hrs”

⸻

5. Peer Community + Mentor Access

🤝 Combine support + connection

	•	Add a built-in community with filters: PLAB 1/2, country, stage
	•	Let advanced users become mentors (verified) and earn perks
	•	Option for 1:1 chat or ask-a-senior-doctor anonymously
	•	“Share my mock score” feature for group accountability

⸻

6. Cultural Context Training for the UK

🇬🇧 What textbooks don’t teach

	•	Mini modules or quizzes on:
	•	NHS structure
	•	Breaking bad news (UK ethics)
	•	GP consultation styles
	•	Common cultural expectations
	•	Language/pronunciation practice for OSCE

⸻

7. Offline Mode + Low-Data Version

🌍 Support users in countries with poor internet

	•	Allow full mock downloads
	•	Option to pre-load PLAB 1 quizzes and flashcards
	•	“Lite mode” with no videos, just text and low-res images
