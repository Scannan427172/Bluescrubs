# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: June 26, 2025**

## Introduction

BlueScrubsPrep ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Professional PLAB Preparation platform.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, username, password
- **Profile Data**: Medical education background, PLAB exam dates, study preferences
- **Contact Information**: Phone number (optional), country of residence
- **Payment Information**: Billing address, payment method details (processed securely through Stripe)

### Usage Data
- **Learning Analytics**: Question performance, study time, progress tracking
- **Technical Data**: IP address, browser type, device information, operating system
- **Interaction Data**: Pages visited, features used, session duration
- **Assessment Results**: Test scores, answer patterns, improvement metrics

### Neurodiversity Accommodation Data
- **Accessibility Preferences**: Font sizes, color schemes, audio settings
- **Learning Accommodations**: Extended time preferences, interface modifications
- **Support Needs**: ADHD, dyslexia, autism, and other neurodiversity accommodations

## How We Use Your Information

### Educational Purposes
- Providing personalized PLAB 1 and PLAB 2 exam preparation
- Generating adaptive learning paths and difficulty adjustments
- Creating AI-powered tutoring and feedback systems
- Tracking progress and providing performance analytics

### Platform Improvement
- Analyzing usage patterns to enhance user experience
- Developing new features and educational content
- Improving accessibility and neurodiversity accommodations
- Optimizing question banks and OSCE station effectiveness

### Communication
- Sending study reminders and achievement notifications
- Providing customer support and technical assistance
- Sharing platform updates and new feature announcements
- Processing subscription and billing communications

## Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

### Legitimate Interest
- Improving educational outcomes for international medical graduates
- Enhancing platform security and preventing fraud
- Conducting analytics to improve service quality
- Providing customer support and technical assistance

### Contractual Necessity
- Creating and managing your user account
- Processing payments and subscriptions
- Delivering educational services and content
- Maintaining platform functionality and access

### Consent
- Sending marketing communications (where required)
- Using optional data for enhanced features
- Sharing anonymized data for research purposes
- Processing special category data with explicit consent

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### Third-Party Service Providers
- **Stripe**: Payment processing (PCI DSS compliant)
- **SendGrid**: Email delivery and communication
- **OpenAI**: AI tutoring features (anonymized data only)
- **Replit**: Platform hosting and infrastructure

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to:
- Comply with court orders or government investigations
- Protect our rights, property, or safety
- Prevent fraud or security threats
- Enforce our terms of service

### No Sale of Personal Data
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for commercial purposes.

## Data Security

### Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption**: All data transmitted using TLS/SSL encryption
- **Database Security**: PostgreSQL with access controls and encryption at rest
- **Authentication**: Secure session management with proper timeout
- **Regular Updates**: Security patches and vulnerability assessments

### Organizational Measures
- **Access Controls**: Limited employee access on need-to-know basis
- **Training**: Regular privacy and security training for staff
- **Incident Response**: Procedures for breach notification and response
- **Regular Audits**: Security assessments and compliance reviews

## Your Rights Under GDPR

### Access Rights
- Request copies of your personal data
- Obtain information about how we process your data
- Receive data in a portable format

### Correction and Deletion
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
- Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests

### Control and Restriction
- Withdraw consent for data processing
- Restrict processing in certain circumstances
- Object to automated decision-making and profiling

### How to Exercise Your Rights
Contact us at privacy@bluescrubsprep.com with:
- Clear identification of your request
- Proof of identity for security purposes
- Specific data or processing you're concerned about

## Data Retention

### Account Data
- **Active Accounts**: Retained while account is active and for legitimate business purposes
- **Inactive Accounts**: Deleted after 3 years of inactivity
- **Learning Data**: Retained for educational improvement and user benefit

### Legal Requirements
- **Payment Records**: Retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance
- **Support Communications**: Retained for 2 years for quality assurance
- **Security Logs**: Retained for 1 year for security monitoring

## International Data Transfers

### Data Location
- Primary servers located in the European Union
- Backup systems in data centers with adequate protection
- Third-party processors comply with GDPR requirements

### Transfer Safeguards
- **Standard Contractual Clauses**: For transfers outside the EU/EEA
- **Adequacy Decisions**: Transfers to countries with adequate protection
- **Binding Corporate Rules**: For multinational service providers

## Children's Privacy

BlueScrubsPrep is designed for medical graduates and professionals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information immediately.
