# Data Protection Policy

**Last Updated: June 26, 2025**

## Introduction

This Data Protection Policy outlines how BlueScrubsPrep implements technical and organizational measures to ensure the security and protection of personal data in compliance with GDPR, UK Data Protection Act 2018, and other applicable privacy regulations.

## Data Protection Principles

### Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency
- All data processing has a clear lawful basis
- Processing purposes clearly communicated to users
- Data collection methods are transparent and fair
- Privacy information provided in clear, understandable language

### Purpose Limitation
- Personal data collected for specified, explicit purposes
- No further processing incompatible with original purposes
- Educational analytics limited to improving learning outcomes
- Medical performance data used solely for exam preparation

### Data Minimization
- Only necessary data collected and processed
- Regular review of data collection practices
- Automatic deletion of unnecessary historical data
- Opt-in approach for non-essential data processing

### Accuracy
- Regular data validation and correction processes
- User ability to update and correct personal information
- Automated checks for data consistency
- Prompt correction of identified inaccuracies

### Storage Limitation
- Clear retention periods for different data categories
- Automatic deletion processes for expired data
- Regular review of retention policies
- Secure disposal of data beyond retention periods

### Integrity and Confidentiality
- Strong technical security measures implemented
- Regular security assessments and updates
- Staff training on data protection requirements
- Incident response procedures for data breaches

## Technical Security Measures

### Encryption
- **Data in Transit**: TLS 1.3 encryption for all communications
- **Data at Rest**: AES-256 encryption for database storage
- **Backup Encryption**: All backups encrypted with separate keys
- **Key Management**: Secure key rotation and storage procedures

### Access Controls
- **Role-Based Access**: Minimum necessary access principles
- **Multi-Factor Authentication**: Required for administrative access
- **Session Management**: Secure timeout and invalidation
- **Audit Logging**: Comprehensive access and activity logs

### Network Security
- **Firewall Protection**: Network segmentation and monitoring
- **Intrusion Detection**: Real-time threat monitoring
- **DDoS Protection**: Automated attack mitigation
- **Vulnerability Scanning**: Regular security assessments

### Application Security
- **Input Validation**: Comprehensive data sanitization
- **SQL Injection Protection**: Parameterized queries and ORM
- **XSS Prevention**: Content Security Policy implementation
- **CSRF Protection**: Token-based request validation

## Organizational Security Measures

### Staff Training
- **Privacy Awareness**: Regular training on data protection principles
- **Security Procedures**: Technical security measure implementation
- **Incident Response**: Breach notification and response procedures
- **Compliance Updates**: Ongoing education on regulatory changes

### Access Management
- **Employee Screening**: Background checks for data access roles
- **Need-to-Know Basis**: Restricted access to personal data
- **Regular Review**: Periodic access right assessments
- **Departure Procedures**: Immediate access revocation upon termination

### Vendor Management
- **Due Diligence**: Security assessment of third-party processors
- **Contractual Safeguards**: Data Processing Agreements with vendors
- **Regular Audits**: Ongoing compliance monitoring
- **Incident Coordination**: Joint breach response procedures

## Data Processing Activities

### User Account Management
- **Purpose**: Platform access and service delivery
- **Legal Basis**: Contract performance
- **Data Categories**: Contact details, authentication data
- **Retention**: Duration of account relationship plus 1 year

### Educational Analytics
- **Purpose**: Learning outcome improvement and personalization
- **Legal Basis**: Legitimate interest
- **Data Categories**: Performance data, study patterns
- **Retention**: 3 years for educational research

### Payment Processing
- **Purpose**: Subscription billing and financial management
- **Legal Basis**: Contract performance and legal obligation
- **Data Categories**: Billing information, payment history
- **Retention**: 7 years for tax and legal compliance

### Customer Support
- **Purpose**: Technical assistance and service improvement
- **Legal Basis**: Contract performance and legitimate interest
- **Data Categories**: Communication logs, issue reports
- **Retention**: 2 years for quality assurance

## Special Category Data

### Neurodiversity Accommodations
- **Data Type**: Health-related accessibility preferences
- **Legal Basis**: Explicit consent
- **Processing Safeguards**: Strict access controls and encryption
- **User Rights**: Withdrawal of consent at any time

### Medical Education Background
- **Data Type**: Educational qualifications and medical training
- **Legal Basis**: Legitimate interest for service customization
- **Processing Safeguards**: Pseudonymization where possible
- **User Rights**: Access, rectification, and erasure

## International Data Transfers

### Transfer Mechanisms
- **EU Standard Contractual Clauses**: For transfers outside EU/EEA
- **Adequacy Decisions**: Transfers to countries with adequate protection
- **Binding Corporate Rules**: For multinational service providers
- **Derogations**: Limited circumstances with explicit consent

### Transfer Safeguards
- **Data Mapping**: Clear documentation of all international transfers
- **Risk Assessment**: Evaluation of destination country protections
- **Additional Safeguards**: Encryption and access controls
- **Regular Review**: Ongoing assessment of transfer arrangements

## Data Subject Rights

### Rights Exercise Process
- **Identity Verification**: Secure authentication before responding
- **Response Timeframe**: 30 days (extendable to 60 days if complex)
- **Free of Charge**: No fees for reasonable requests
- **Communication**: Clear, plain language responses

### Right of Access
- **Information Provided**: Data categories, purposes, recipients
- **Data Copy**: Portable format where technically feasible
- **Additional Information**: Retention periods, rights information
- **Limitations**: Protection of third-party rights and trade secrets

### Rectification and Erasure
- **Correction Process**: Prompt rectification of inaccurate data
- **Erasure Grounds**: No longer necessary, consent withdrawal, unlawful processing
- **Technical Implementation**: Secure deletion from all systems
- **Third-Party Notification**: Informing recipients of corrections/erasure

### Restriction and Objection
- **Processing Restriction**: Limiting processing to storage only
- **Objection Rights**: Opt-out for direct marketing and legitimate interest processing
- **Automated Decision-Making**: Right to human intervention
- **Profiling**: Information about logic and significance

## Data Breach Management

### Detection and Assessment
- **Monitoring Systems**: Automated breach detection tools
- **Incident Classification**: Risk assessment based on nature and scope
- **Impact Evaluation**: Assessment of harm to data subjects
- **Documentation**: Detailed incident logs and investigation records

### Notification Procedures
- **Supervisory Authority**: 72-hour notification requirement
- **Data Subject Notification**: Direct communication if high risk
- **Communication Content**: Nature of breach, likely consequences, measures taken
- **Follow-up**: Ongoing updates during investigation

### Response Measures
- **Immediate Containment**: Steps to limit ongoing harm
- **Investigation**: Root cause analysis and impact assessment
- **Remediation**: Measures to address vulnerabilities
- **Prevention**: Implementation of additional safeguards

## Compliance Monitoring

### Regular Audits
- **Internal Audits**: Quarterly data protection compliance reviews
- **External Assessments**: Annual third-party security audits
- **Penetration Testing**: Regular security vulnerability assessments
- **Compliance Reporting**: Ongoing monitoring of regulatory requirements

### Data Protection Impact Assessments
- **DPIA Triggers**: High-risk processing identification
- **Assessment Process**: Systematic evaluation of privacy risks
- **Mitigation Measures**: Implementation of risk reduction strategies
- **Regular Review**: Ongoing assessment of processing activities

### Record Keeping
- **Processing Records**: Article 30 GDPR compliance documentation
- **Consent Records**: Clear evidence of consent and withdrawal
- **Training Records**: Staff education and competency documentation
- **Audit Trails**: Comprehensive logging of data access and processing
