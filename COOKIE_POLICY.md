# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated: June 26, 2025**

## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit BlueScrubsPrep. They help us provide essential functionality, improve your experience, and understand how you use our Professional PLAB Preparation platform.

## How We Use Cookies

### Essential Cookies (Always Active)
These cookies are necessary for the platform to function and cannot be disabled.

**Authentication & Security**
- `session_id`: Maintains your login session
- `csrf_token`: Protects against cross-site request forgery
- `auth_state`: Manages authentication status

**Platform Functionality** 
- `user_preferences`: Stores accessibility settings and neurodiversity accommodations
- `language_setting`: Remembers your selected language
- `theme_preference`: Saves light/dark mode choice

### Performance Cookies (Optional)
Help us understand platform usage and improve performance.

**Analytics**
- `analytics_session`: Tracks session duration for platform optimization
- `page_views`: Monitors popular content for improvement
- `error_tracking`: Identifies technical issues for resolution

**Learning Analytics**
- `study_progress`: Tracks PLAB preparation advancement
- `question_performance`: Monitors accuracy and improvement
- `adaptive_learning`: Enables personalized difficulty adjustment

### Functional Cookies (Optional)
Enhance your experience with additional features.

**Study Tools**
- `bookmarked_questions`: Saves favorite questions for review
- `recent_topics`: Remembers recently studied medical specialties
- `practice_settings`: Stores quiz preferences and timer settings

**Communication**
- `notification_prefs`: Manages email and platform notification settings
- `support_chat`: Maintains customer support conversation context

## Third-Party Cookies

### Payment Processing (Stripe)
- `stripe_session`: Secure payment processing
- `payment_method`: Saved payment preferences (with consent)

### Email Services (SendGrid)
- `email_tracking`: Delivery confirmation for important notifications
- `unsubscribe_status`: Honors email preference changes

### AI Services (OpenAI)
- No cookies set directly; API calls are server-side only

## Cookie Management

### Browser Controls
**Chrome**: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies
**Firefox**: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data  
**Safari**: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and Website Data
**Edge**: Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions

### Platform Controls
Access cookie preferences through:
1. Account Settings > Privacy Preferences
2. Cookie banner on first visit
3. Footer privacy links

### Granular Control
- **Accept All**: Enable all cookies for full functionality
- **Essential Only**: Use only necessary cookies
- **Custom Settings**: Choose specific cookie categories
- **Reject All**: Minimal functionality with essential cookies only

## Cookie Retention Periods

### Session Cookies
- **Duration**: Deleted when browser closes
- **Purpose**: Temporary functionality during your visit
- **Examples**: Shopping cart, form data, session authentication

### Persistent Cookies
- **Authentication**: 30 days (or until logout)
- **Preferences**: 1 year (or until manually changed)
- **Analytics**: 2 years (anonymized after 14 months)
- **Study Progress**: Duration of account lifetime
